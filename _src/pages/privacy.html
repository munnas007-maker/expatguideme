This policy explains what information ExpatGuideME (expatguideme.com) uses and why. We keep it short and plain.

What is stored in your browser

The site saves a few small settings in your browser's local storage so it works the way you left it:

  • your chosen language and light or dark mode;
  • the currencies you last picked and a copy of the latest exchange rates, so the page loads faster;
  • that you clicked "OK" on the cookie notice.

These settings stay on your device. We cannot see them, and you can remove them at any time by clearing your browser's site data.

Forms: newsletter and contact

If you subscribe to our newsletter, we receive your email address. If you use the contact form, we receive your name, email address and message. We use this only to send you updates you asked for or to reply to you. We never sell or share it for marketing.

Form submissions are processed and stored by our hosting provider, Netlify. To unsubscribe or ask us to delete your details, send a message through the contact page.

Please do not send passwords, ID numbers or bank details through any form.

Outside services the site uses

  • Netlify hosts the website. Like any web server, it processes technical data such as your IP address to deliver pages and keep the site secure.
  • Google Fonts provides the fonts. Your browser downloads them from Google's servers.
  • ExchangeRate-API provides the exchange rates. On the exchange rate page, your browser requests the latest rates directly from their servers.

Each of these services has its own privacy policy.

Advertising and cookies

The site does not use advertising or tracking cookies today. In future we may show ads, for example from Google AdSense, to keep the site free. If we do, those ad services may use cookies, and we will update this page and the cookie notice before that happens.

Links to other websites

We link to official websites and other useful services. Links to other websites include a tag that tells them the visit came from ExpatGuideME. Their privacy practices are their own.

Children

The site is meant for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date below. For any privacy question, please use the contact page.
