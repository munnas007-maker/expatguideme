ExpatGuideME is free to use. To pay for hosting, research and new tools, we earn money in two ways. This page explains both, so you always know when we may benefit.

Advertising

We show ads, for example through Google AdSense. Ads are placed in clearly marked "Advertisement" spaces. Advertisers do not choose or approve what we write. You can read how ad cookies work in our privacy policy.

Partner (affiliate) links

Some links to banks, money transfer services or other products may be partner links. If you sign up or use a service through a partner link, we may receive a commission. This does not cost you anything extra.

  • Partner links are always labelled "Partner".
  • Being a partner never changes the information we show, the rates in our tables or the order of providers. On the money transfer page, providers are ordered by the amount your family receives.
  • We list providers whether or not they are partners. Today, none of the links on the site are partner links.

Our promise

We only recommend checking services that are licensed in their country, and we always tell you to compare the final amount and confirm details in the official app or website. If you think something on the site is unfair or wrong, please tell us on the contact page.
