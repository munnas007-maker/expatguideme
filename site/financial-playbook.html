1. Know what your gratuity is based on

In Saudi Arabia, end of service is calculated on your last full wage, including fixed allowances such as housing. In the UAE and Qatar it is based on your basic salary only. If your basic salary is low compared with your allowances, your gratuity will be smaller than you may expect, so check the split in your contract.

2. Compare what arrives, not the fee

A transfer with no fee can still cost more if the exchange rate is weaker. Before each transfer, enter the same amount in two or three apps and choose the one where your family receives the most. Our free send money tool does this comparison for you.

3. Build a safety cushion first

Job changes and family emergencies happen. A common rule of thumb is to keep 3 to 6 months of family expenses in savings you can reach quickly, before taking on long loans or investments back home.
