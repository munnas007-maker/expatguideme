Frequently asked questions

Why does my bank or transfer app give a different rate?

This page shows the market (mid) rate. Banks and transfer apps add a small margin and sometimes a fee, so you receive a little less. Always compare the final amount your family will receive.

How often are the rates updated?

The rates come from an international exchange-rate service and update about once a day. The time of the last update is shown under the result.

Is the Saudi riyal fixed to the US dollar?

Yes. The Saudi riyal is pegged at 3.75 riyals to 1 US dollar. The Qatari riyal, Omani rial and Bahraini dinar are also pegged to the dollar, while the Kuwaiti dinar follows a basket of currencies. So the changes you see usually come from your home currency moving.

Is this an official or bank website?

No. ExpatGuideME is an independent website. For sending money, use your bank or a licensed transfer service, and check their rate and fee before you send.
