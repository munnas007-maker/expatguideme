Stay safe

  • Book appointments only on official websites. Embassies warn about agents who charge for free appointments.
  • Never hand over your passport to anyone except the embassy, its official centre or the authorities.
  • Keep photos of your passport, residence ID and contract on your phone and with your family.

Frequently asked questions

Can I renew my passport while living in the Gulf?

Yes. Indian, Pakistani and Nepali citizens can renew passports through their embassy or its official centre, and Pakistanis can also renew online. Start well before your residence renewal, because many Gulf services need a valid passport.

Which embassy should I contact?

Contact your own country's embassy or consulate in the Gulf country where you live. In Saudi Arabia, some services are handled by a consulate in Jeddah for the western region.

Is this an official website?

No. ExpatGuideME is independent. We link to official websites so you can confirm fees, documents and appointment rules before you go.
