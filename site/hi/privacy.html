संक्षेप में: हम सिर्फ़ आपकी भाषा और सेटिंग्स आपके ब्राउज़र में सहेजते हैं, आपका ईमेल सिर्फ़ जवाब देने या आपकी माँगी जानकारी भेजने के लिए इस्तेमाल करते हैं, और Google व उसके पार्टनर विज्ञापन दिखाने के लिए कुकीज़ का उपयोग कर सकते हैं। पूरी नीति नीचे अंग्रेज़ी में है।

मोबाइल ऐप लॉन्च सूची

अगर आप मोबाइल ऐप पेज पर ऐप लॉन्च सूची में जुड़ते हैं, तो हमें सिर्फ़ आपका ईमेल पता और चुनी गई भाषा मिलती है। हम इनका उपयोग केवल यह बताने के लिए करते हैं कि ExpatGuideME ऐप Google Play और App Store पर कब उपलब्ध है। हम आपका ईमेल कभी किसी को बेचेंगे, किराए पर देंगे या साझा नहीं करेंगे, और आप संपर्क पेज से कभी भी इसे हटाने को कह सकते हैं।

विशेषज्ञ जाँच के अनुरोध

अगर आप विशेषज्ञ जाँच का अनुरोध करते हैं, तो हमें आपका नाम, ईमेल, देश, विषय और संदेश मिलता है। हम इनका उपयोग सिर्फ़ आपके अनुरोध का जवाब देने के लिए करते हैं और, अगर आप फ़ॉर्म पर सहमति दें, तो मदद कर सकने वाले एक स्वतंत्र सलाहकार को भेजते हैं। हम आपकी जानकारी कभी नहीं बेचते। आईडी नंबर, पासवर्ड या बैंक विवरण न भेजें। आप संपर्क पेज से कभी भी अपना अनुरोध हटाने को कह सकते हैं।

प्लेबुक प्रतीक्षा सूची और ख़रीद

अगर आप फ़ाइनेंशियल प्लेबुक की प्रतीक्षा सूची में जुड़ते हैं, तो हम आपका ईमेल सिर्फ़ यह बताने के लिए उपयोग करते हैं कि गाइड कब उपलब्ध है। भुगतान, भुगतान के समय दिखने वाली चेकआउट सेवा संभालती है; हम आपके कार्ड का विवरण कभी नहीं देखते।

This policy explains what information ExpatGuideME (expatguideme.com) uses and why. We keep it short and plain.

What is stored in your browser

The site saves a few small settings in your browser's local storage so it works the way you left it:

  • your chosen language and light or dark mode;
  • the currencies you last picked and a copy of the latest exchange rates, so the page loads faster;
  • that you clicked "OK" on the cookie notice;
  • the country, options and amounts you last used in our calculators. Salary and cost figures are not saved.

These settings stay on your device. We cannot see them, and you can remove them at any time by clearing your browser's site data.

Forms: newsletter and contact

If you subscribe to our newsletter, we receive your email address. If you use the contact form, we receive your name, email address and message. We use this only to send you updates you asked for or to reply to you. We never sell or share it for marketing.

Form submissions are sent to us by email through FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), a form-to-email service. To unsubscribe or ask us to delete your details, send a message through the contact page.

Please do not send passwords, ID numbers or bank details through any form.

Mobile app launch list

If you join the app launch list on our Mobile app page, we receive only your email address and your chosen language. We use them solely to tell you when the ExpatGuideME app is available in Google Play and the App Store. We will never sell, rent or share your email address with anyone. You can ask us to delete it at any time through the contact page.

Expert review requests

If you use the Ask an expert form, we receive your name, email address, the country you work in, the topic and your message. We use them only to reply to you and, if you tick the consent box, to pass your request to one independent advisor who may be able to help. We never sell your details. Do not send ID numbers, passwords or bank details. You can ask us to delete your request at any time through the contact page.

Financial Playbook waitlist and purchases

If you join the Financial Playbook waitlist, we use your email address only to tell you when the guide is available. If you buy the guide, payment is handled by the checkout provider named on the payment page; we never see or store your card details.

Outside services the site uses

  • GitHub Pages hosts the website. Like any web server, it processes technical data such as your IP address to deliver pages and keep the site secure.
  • FormSubmit (formsubmit.co) delivers the messages you send through our forms to our email inbox.
  • Google Fonts provides the fonts. Your browser downloads them from Google's servers.
  • ExchangeRate-API provides the exchange rates. On the exchange rate page, your browser requests the latest rates directly from their servers.

Each of these services has its own privacy policy.

Advertising and cookies

We show ads to keep the site free. Ads may be served by Google AdSense and other advertising partners.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your earlier visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies lets Google and its partners serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the internet.
  • You can turn off personalised advertising in Google's Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at www.aboutads.info.
  • To learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Where the law requires it, for example for visitors in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, ads are shown only after you give consent through a consent message, and you can change your choice at any time.

Some links to services may be affiliate links. If you use one, the partner may set a cookie to record that you came from our site. See our affiliate disclosure.

Links to other websites

We link to official websites and other useful services. Links to other websites include a tag that tells them the visit came from ExpatGuideME. Their privacy practices are their own.

Children

The site is meant for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date below. For any privacy question, please use the contact page.
