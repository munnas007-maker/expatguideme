ExpatGuideME (expatguideme.com) is an independent website run by expats who live and work in the Gulf. We build free tools and write simple guides for the people we know best: workers and families from India, Pakistan, Nepal, Bangladesh, Sri Lanka, the Philippines and beyond who live in Saudi Arabia, the UAE, Kuwait, Qatar, Oman and Bahrain.

Why we built it

Official websites are the final word, but they are often in one or two languages and hard to follow. Many expats end up asking friends, paying agents or trusting social media posts. ExpatGuideME explains the basics in plain English, Arabic, Hindi, Malayalam, Nepali, Urdu, Bengali and Tagalog, and then links to the official website so you can confirm the final answer yourself.

What you will find here

  • Exchange rates from Gulf currencies to your home currency.
  • A money transfer comparison for each Gulf country, with rates shared by other expats.
  • An end-of-service benefit calculator based on each country's labour law.
  • Guides to ID cards and residency and embassy services, and a family cost calculator.

How we check information

We base every guide on official sources such as labour laws, government portals and embassy websites, and on reliable news reports when rules change. Each page shows the date it was last checked. Rules and fees can change without notice, so we always tell you where to confirm them.

Found a mistake? Please tell us on the contact page. We correct errors as soon as we can.

We are not a government website

ExpatGuideME is not connected to any government office, embassy, bank or exchange company. We never ask for passwords, ID numbers or bank details.

How the site is paid for

The site is free to use. We pay for it with advertising, for example Google AdSense, and with partner links that are always marked "Partner". Advertising never changes what we write. Read our affiliate disclosure and privacy policy.
