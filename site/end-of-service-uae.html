End of service calculator for the UAE (2026)

How much gratuity (end-of-service award) you get in the UAE, with worked examples and the official rules.

The rule in one sentence

After at least one year of service: 21 days' basic salary for each of the first five years, then 30 days' basic salary for each year after that. Part years count in proportion. The total cannot be more than two years' wage.

It is worked out on your basic salary only (not housing or other allowances). Source: Article 51 of the UAE Labour Law.

When you must be paid

Within 14 days after the contract ends. If your employer does not pay, you can complain to the Ministry of Human Resources and Emiratisation (MOHRE).

Frequently asked questions

How is end of service calculated in the UAE?

Under Article 51 of the UAE Labour Law: After at least one year of service: 21 days' basic salary for each of the first five years, then 30 days' basic salary for each year after that. Part years count in proportion. The total cannot be more than two years' wage. It is worked out on your basic salary only (not housing or other allowances).

Do I lose my gratuity if I resign in the UAE?

Resigning does not reduce the gratuity in the UAE, as long as you have at least one year of service.
