المواضع الممولة وعروض الشركاء ودليلنا الخاص

قد تدفع بعض شركات التحويل لتظهر بوصف «شريك مموَّل»، فتحصل على علامة وصف مميز، لكنها تبقى في مكانها نفسه في الجدول الذي يُرتَّب دائمًا حسب ما تستلمه عائلتك. وبطاقة «أساسيات المقيم» تحتوي على روابط ممولة فقط. أما الدليل المالي فهو دليلنا المدفوع، ونوضح ذلك في صفحته.

ExpatGuideME is free to use. To pay for hosting, research and new tools, we earn money from advertising, partner links, sponsored placements and our own paid guide. This page explains each one, so you always know when we may benefit.

Advertising

We show ads, for example through Google AdSense. Ads are placed in clearly marked "Advertisement" spaces. Advertisers do not choose or approve what we write. You can read how ad cookies work in our privacy policy.

Partner (affiliate) links

Some links to banks, money transfer services or other products may be partner links. If you sign up or use a service through a partner link, we may receive a commission. This does not cost you anything extra.

  • Partner links are marked as sponsored links, and a note under the money transfer table explains them whenever they are active.
  • Being a partner never changes the information we show, the rates in our tables or the order of providers. On the money transfer page, providers are ordered by the amount your family receives.
  • We list providers whether or not they are partners.

Sponsored placements

A money transfer provider may pay to be shown as a "Sponsored partner". It gets a label and a highlighted row, but it stays in the same position in the table, which is always ordered by the amount your family receives. The "Expat essentials" card at the bottom of our pages (mobile data, insurance, relocation) contains sponsored links only and is labelled as such.

Our own products and services

The Financial Playbook is our own paid guide, and its page says so. The Ask an expert form can connect you with an independent advisor; ExpatGuideME is not a law firm, and the advisor sets their own fees.

Our promise

We only recommend checking services that are licensed in their country, and we always tell you to compare the final amount and confirm details in the official app or website. If you think something on the site is unfair or wrong, please tell us on the contact page.
